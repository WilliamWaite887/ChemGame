# Machine pixel-material generation briefs

These four original masters were created with Codex's built-in image generation
mode, then reduced to 128 x 128 with nearest-neighbor sampling. The shared brief
requested seamless, orthographic, flat-albedo, deliberately low-resolution
pixel art matching the station surfaces, with no copied sprites, text, logos,
watermarks, perspective, structural panel borders, bolts, stripes, directional
lighting, gradients, deep shadows, gloss, or photorealism.

- `machine_casing.png`: Medium graphite-grey painted casing with subtle hard-edged
  pixel wear, tiny chips, sparse short scratches, and extremely faint mottling.
- `machine_casing_light.png`: Cool light-grey trim with restrained pixel speckles,
  tiny dark chips, sparse scuffs, and rare off-white highlight pixels.
- `machine_dark_metal.png`: Near-black charcoal face panels with quiet blocky grain,
  sparse graphite flecks, and tiny scratches so screens remain dominant.
- `machine_steel.png`: Mid-tone cool steel with restrained horizontal pixel brushing,
  sparse darker nicks, and small pale steel flecks.
